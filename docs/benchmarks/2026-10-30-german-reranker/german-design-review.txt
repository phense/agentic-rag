## Corpus and denominator review

Read all 32 case definitions and their labels: 16 German questions and 16
faithful English counterparts, eight authored problem families, two question
pairs per family. Each case has six public invented candidate passages; the
maximum passage length is 138 characters. The positive labels are unambiguous
in the final corpus. Translated names and monetary formatting preserve the
relationships and relevance targets. Questions cover explicit denial, compounds,
inflection, amount thresholds, approved periods, paraphrases, grammatical roles,
two-source conditions and domain ambiguity. These are short controlled probes,
not representative natural-language traffic or an established German benchmark.

Primary quality uses only permutation zero: n=16 questions per language/model,
17 expected source occurrences per language, and eight related families. The
second permutation reverses the same deterministic seeded candidate order and
is a position-robustness check; it must not double the independent denominator.
Corresponding German/English pairs receive identical positional permutations.
Binary top1, macro recall@3, complete-source coverage@3 and nDCG@3 formulas
match these labels. Scores do not become probabilities of truth.

The loop produces 128 semantic observations
(32 questions × two permutations × two models), plus 32 actual adapter probes
(16 German questions × two models), exactly 160 retained rows. Raw inference
errors remain in rows and fail the run; adapter failures retain the original
order and their fixed diagnostic/error evidence. Reporting must distinguish
ranked outcomes from fallback quality. All cases are answerable, so this
experiment does not establish unsupported-query abstention.

## Finding resolved before any inference

**Medium, label ambiguity:** `multi-condition-1-de/en` required passages 0 and 1,
but passage 3 initially named both approval and handover report in its statement
that a viewing replaces neither. It could therefore answer the required-document
question despite being labelled irrelevant. The coordinator removed that clause
in both languages and both sibling case copies. Passage 3 now only states that
a viewing may be arranged in advance; this still uniquely answers question 2.
The final corpus no longer contains the ambiguous clause. No model results
preceded this correction; it is not post-result label adjustment.

Three method clarifications are also resolved in the frozen definitions:

- The raw `httpx` value 30 is a per-operation HTTP timeout for measurement,
  rather than a strict total-wall deadline. The unchanged production adapter
  retains its real 1.5-second total deadline.
- Adapter probes immediately follow a raw call for the same case and use short
  passages. They are warm repeated-case probes, not evidence for cold or
  representative six-by-600-character production latency.
- The conservative decision rule now explicitly rejects a blanket German
  recommendation on any first-permutation German top1 miss in the
  `access-negation` or `subject-object` families. This avoids a subjective later
  distinction between wrong-topic results and semantic contradictions. Two
  overall German misses or a German/English gap above ten percentage points
  are additional stated rejection rules. Passing only supports a larger local
  acceptance trial; it cannot establish general suitability.

## Helper and ownership review

The helper validates its fixed corpus and query eligibility, freezes its own
bytes/corpus plus original source hashes before calls, checks both pinned GGUF
checksums and the previously measured native runtime checksum, records native
version/libraries, and checks those identities again after measurement. Labels
and expected IDs enter only local scoring, not the native request payload.
Raw responses require the advertised alias, all six distinct indexes and finite
scores in [0,1]; original adapter objects remain the source of returned ordering.

The required combined unit verifies four-CPU quota/affinity, 8 GiB memory and
zero swap. Model copies are created within that unit. Only fresh output/work
paths are accepted; model child handles are recorded immediately on startup.
The helper checks model health/alias, actual child affinity and same-cgroup
membership. It uses literal owned loopback ports 8772/8773, no database and no
hosted provider. Reused `/slots` draining waits for native idle between calls,
including after timeout. Cleanup verifies ownership, independently stops every
tracked child, treats forced termination as failure, checks all remaining cgroup
members and writes an explicit exit artifact. External collected-unit/port
cleanup inspection remains required after the actual run.

No additional model, alternate provider, production configuration or migration
is introduced. Shared-host and tiny authored-sample limits must remain visible
in the final report. No production adoption or PR merge is authorized here.
