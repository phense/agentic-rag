six questions in both permutations. Exact native JSON in rejected-c shows
finite negative relevance scores, for example -0.01824083924293518. Their
old 0..1 validation therefore rejected otherwise structurally complete native
ordering output. Both old helper exits are 1; those attempts cannot satisfy
successful comparison gates.

Current raw semantic scoring accepts any finite scalar score while preserving
alias, size, complete distinct index, type and finiteness validation. It does
not normalize, clamp or interpret scores as probabilities. Exact response bytes
are retained before validation. A separate per-row range-compatibility flag
reports whether the unchanged product adapter could accept these raw scores.
All first-permutation English cases now also call the actual product adapter;
no production guard or deadline was relaxed.

This correction changes the diagnostic's measurement contract, not its corpus,
labels, candidate model, rejection threshold or target outcomes. All questions
and the conservative German recommendation rule remain byte-identical to the
premeasurement corpus, so the negative-score discovery is not held-out retuning.

## Frozen decision and concrete failures

All three rejection predicates hold: six German primary top1 misses; misses
in both critical families; German top1 trails English by 12.5 percentage points.
The critical cases are access-negation-2-de and subject-object-2-de. The first
selects a pending photo-archive request instead of the explicitly denied invoice
archive access; it is a wrong-topic result, not evidence that authorization
was changed. The second reverses the requested grammatical relationship:
it selects Mara reviewing Levin's expenses when asked whose expenses Levin
reviews (correct evidence: Levin reviewing Nora).

Other German first-place failures are exact amount threshold, October-only
approval, arranging a viewing and database-recovery paraphrase. The database
case ranks password recovery first and the required database evidence fifth,
outside top3. Source-ranking quality, including these failures, is consistently
retained. A small hand-authored paired sample cannot establish population
language accuracy or prove English training as the cause of any particular miss.
