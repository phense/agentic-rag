## Terms and limits

Primary: first seeded permutation. Scored: diagnostic native response available. Censored: attempted raw request without usable response; metrics unavailable. Unattempted: no request. Ranked: product accepted ranking within its deadline. Fallback: product retains original order. Macro Recall@3: per-question fraction of required sources in the first 3, averaged. All-sources@3: every required source is in the first 3. MRR: reciprocal rank of the first required source. nDCG@3: discounted required-source ranking normalized by the ideal first 3. Server RSS and whole-cgroup peak cover different memory scopes.

The sigmoid(logit) and fixed cosine-affine bridges preserve observed native order and are uncalibrated transport scores. v3.5 FP32/Transformers versus published INT8 ONNX are distinct deployable configurations; results are not precision-equivalent or architecture-only comparisons. v2 and v3.5 CC BY-NC weights require separate commercial-license consideration; L6/mMARCO Apache-2.0 does not preapprove adoption. Related synthetic controls support no population accuracy, retrieval recall, answer accuracy, private-corpus, GPU/Mac, full advertised-context or production-readiness claim. v3.5 measured infeasibility under this declared envelope is a result, not an application defect to tune away.
